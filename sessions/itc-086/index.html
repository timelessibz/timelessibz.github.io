Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC086

Vocal Trance Classics Legendary Voices of 90s & 2000s Trance

ITC086

This session is for the moments when trance found its voice.

When melodies weren’t enough… and lyrics went straight to your soul. This is classic vocal trance at its purest, a journey through the golden era where emotion, energy and unforgettable vocals ruled the dancefloor.

From the first atmospheric intro of SASH! – Le Soleil Noir to the hands-in-the-air euphoria of Castles In The Sky, this mix captures the magic of late 90s and early 2000s vocal trance — when every breakdown felt personal, and every chorus stayed with you forever.

These tracks remind us of: - Ibiza nights turning into sunrise - Club lights fading as the melody carried you away - That moment when the vocal dropped… and the crowd became one
