Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC088

Euphoric 90s Ibiza Anthems & Melodic Trance

There was a moment in Ibiza when trance felt endless. When melodies floated over the sea, the sun rose behind the DJ booth, and every track felt like freedom.

Ibiza Trance Classics 088 captures that late-90s / early-2000s sound — melodic, progressive, uplifting and deeply atmospheric. The era when trance wasn’t just energy… it was emotion, space and journey.

From the Balearic warmth of Too Much Rain and Hear You Calling to the euphoric lift of Wings Of Love, this session flows through timeless Ibiza dancefloor memories — sunrise sets, open-air terraces and hands-in-the-air moments.

This is the sound of: Ibiza mornings after endless nights Melodies drifting over the Mediterranean Trance at its most pure and emotional
