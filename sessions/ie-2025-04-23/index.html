Ibiza Specials

The Best Eurodance 90s 2000s

DJ Set [Snap! Culture Beat, 2 Unlimited, Eiffel 65, Bellini]

Feel the Pulse of 90s Eurodance

Get ready to travel back to the golden age of eurodance! This high-energy session brings you a nonstop mix of the biggest club anthems from the 1990s. From unforgettable synths to booming basslines and iconic vocals, this mix is made for true lovers of the dancefloor. Relive the sound of a generation with hits from acts like Snap!, Technotronic, 2 Brothers on the 4th Floor, and many more.

Whether you're working out, dancing at home, or just vibing, this mix will lift your spirit and make you move. Let the rhythm take control!
