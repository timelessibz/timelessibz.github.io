Ibiza Trance Classics

90s–2000s Full Power Vinyl Set

Trance That Gave You Goosebumps

This is the sound that gave us goosebumps. This isn’t just a mix — it’s an emotional flashback to a time when trance music made us feel invincible. Prepare for a 1-hour full-power set featuring some of the most iconic, euphoric and emotionally charged tracks from the golden years of trance: 1997–2006.

These are the drops, the breakdowns, and the melodies that made us believe in something bigger If you’ve ever raised your hands with your eyes closed on a dancefloor… you’ll feel this one.

Featuring classics from: Above & Beyond, Paul van Dyk, Push, System F, Armin van Buuren, Chicane, and many more.
