Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC072

Euphoric 2000s Anthems

Svenson & Gielen, DuMonde, Anjunabeats

Feel the magic of Ibiza once again with IBIZA TRANCE CLASSICS 072 A euphoric journey through pure classic trance anthems that shaped the golden era — filled with emotion, melody, and the spirit of the 2000s dancefloors.

Featuring legends like Svenson & Gielen, DuMonde, Vincent De Moor, Anjunabeats, Boccaccio Life, Celesta, Atlantique, and more. Perfect for trance lovers who miss that uplifting, emotional energy that defined an entire generation.

Let the sound take you back to Ibiza’s sunsets, lasers, and unforgettable nights.
