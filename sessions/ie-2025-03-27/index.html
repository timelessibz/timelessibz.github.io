Ibiza Specials

Classic Trance Anthems (2000-2011)

Ramsterdam, Daniel Kandi, Kyau & Albert, Omnia, Goldenscan

Relive the golden era of trance! From 2002 to 2011, trance music reached new heights, blending uplifting melodies, euphoric breakdowns, and powerful basslines that defined a generation. Whether you were raving at festivals, listening to A State of Trance, or discovering the magic of progressive and vocal trance, this mix will take you back to those unforgettable moments.

This era gave us legendary anthems from the biggest names in trance: Armin van Buuren, Tiësto, Ferry Corsten, Paul van Dyk, Above & Beyond, Rank 1, Cosmic Gate, ATB, and many more. Their productions shaped the sound of modern electronic music and continue to inspire new generations of trance lovers.

What to expect in this mix? Timeless trance classics from 2002-2011 Uplifting & emotional melodies that touch the soul Powerful progressive basslines that energize your spirit Vocal anthems that give you goosebumps A euphoric journey through one of trance’s most iconic decades

Whether you're working, driving, studying, or simply closing your eyes and losing yourself in the music, this mix is designed to bring pure emotion and energy straight to your speakers!
