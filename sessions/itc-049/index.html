Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC049

1993–2004 Summer Set ft. Cosmic Gate Ian Van Dahl Pulsedriver

ITC049

Reconnect with the raw energy and hypnotic vibes of trance's golden era. This set features deeper and darker classics from 1993 to 2004, with legends like Ian van Dahl, La Luna, Rodd-Y-Ler and Kai Tracid. A tribute to the underground clubs, early-morning sets, and late-night euphoria that defined a generation.

Perfect for introspection, deep focus, or transporting your mind back to the underground.
