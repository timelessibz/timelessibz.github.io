Trance Classics Reworked · ITCR002

The Best Classic Trance Remixed for Today!

Armin van Buuren,Push

Get ready for an electrifying journey through the best classic trance anthems, reworked and remixed by today's top artists! This mix brings the nostalgia of the late '90s and early 2000s trance era back to life with fresh, modern energy. Whether you love uplifting trance, progressive trance, or tech-trance, this session is crafted to keep you in a state of pure euphoria.

Featuring incredible remixes and reworks from Armin van Buuren, Giuseppe Ottaviani, Ferry Corsten, Andrew Rayel, Aly & Fila, Factor B, Bryan Kearney, Will Atkinson, and more! If you're a fan of A State of Trance (ASOT) or FSOE, this set is for you.

Enjoy the power of trance classics like never before! Let me know in the comments which track is your favorite!
