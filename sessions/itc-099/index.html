Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC099

Forgotten 90s & 2000s Trance Gems

Lost In Ibiza

LOST IN IBIZA... Some tracks become hits. Others become memories. This episode of Ibiza Trance Classics is dedicated to the hidden gems, forgotten anthems and underground treasures that helped define the sound of Ibiza's golden era. From Plastic Boy and K90 to Scott Mac, Adam Sheridan and Soulfighter, this mix takes you on a journey through the deeper side of late 90s and early 2000s trance. A soundtrack for sunsets, road trips and memories that never fade.
