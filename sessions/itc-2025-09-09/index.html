Ibiza Trance Classics

Balearic Dreams Emotional 90s & 2000s Trance Set

Balearic sunsets, warm melodies, timeless trance. This 1-hour Ibiza Trance Classics mix takes you deep into the more emotional and chill side of trance from the late 90s and early 2000s.

With tracks remixed by legends like Chicane, Tiësto, Thrillseekers, Vincent de Moor, Dumonde & Signum, this set flows between Balearic textures, vocal layers, and deep emotional energy.

Perfect for sunsets, late-night drives, or moments of pure nostalgia.
