Ibiza Specials

Best of Eurodance 90sNonstop Summer Party Mix

Classic Hits & Dancefloor Anthems

Let’s dance like it's 1995! Get ready for a high-energy trip back to the golden age of Eurodance! This one-hour nonstop summer mix brings you the most iconic hits of the 90s — the soundtrack of school discos, summer holidays, and unforgettable nights on the dancefloor. From infectious choruses to punchy synths and powerful female vocals, this session is pure nostalgia, designed to make you move and sing along. Perfect for beach parties, retro nights, or your next workout playlist.

Featuring legends like 2 Unlimited, Snap!, La Bouche, Culture Beat, Corona & more!
