Ibiza House Classics · IHC001

Sunset House Anthems

The Golden Era of Ibiza

Welcome to the very first episode of Ibiza House Classics!

Travel back to the golden age of Ibiza, where sunsets, beach clubs and unforgettable house music created memories that still live on today.

This series celebrates the legendary house anthems that defined the island from the late 90s through the early 2000s. From soulful vocals and funky grooves to timeless dancefloor classics, every episode is a tribute to the soundtrack of Ibiza's most iconic summers.

Whether you experienced those magical nights or you're discovering them for the first time, this mix is your ticket back to the White Isle.
