Best of 1998–2001 Trance Anthems ft. Mac Zimms, Rin Hagen & More

ITC042

Feel the pulse of a legendary era with this powerful trance session straight from the golden years of 1999–2003. Featuring emotional builds, soaring synths, and unforgettable drops, this mix brings you the best uplifting anthems from pioneers like ATB, Rank 1, and more.

Perfect for reminiscing late-night raves, Ibiza sunsets, and timeless club moments.
