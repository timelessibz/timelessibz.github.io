Trance Classics Mashups

Airscape & Svenson x Factor B & Cat Martin - L'Esperanza x Crasing Over (GRK Mashup)

This is a unique mashup blending two legendary classic trance anthems from the 90s and 2000s! If you love 90s trance classics, uplifting beats, and euphoric melodies, this remix will transport you straight to the trance golden age mix era!

We've combined the energy of Airscape - L'Esperanza with the magic of Factor B & Cat Martin - Crashin Over, creating a powerful fusion that pays tribute to the best classic trance songs of all time. Inspired by Ministry of Sound 90s anthems, this mix brings together the best elements of techno trance music best hits and the most beautiful trance songs.

Original artists featured: Airscape & Svenson x Factor B & Cat Martin Labels: Armada, Vandit, Bonzai, Anjunabeats, Black Hole, Platipus For fans of: Paul van Dyk, Ferry Corsten, Tiësto, Rank 1, ATB, Cosmic Gate, System F, Solarstone
