Armin van Buuren Tribute Mix The Early Years

CHRISTMAS SPECIAL – ARMIN VAN BUUREN ONLY

This Christmas I’m celebrating with a special session dedicated exclusively to Armin, revisiting his most emotional, melodic and timeless trance sound — the sound that defined an era in Ibiza and shaped the history of trance music.

This mix is part of the Ibiza Trance Classics project, a musical journey through the golden years of trance, designed to be enjoyed from start to finish.
