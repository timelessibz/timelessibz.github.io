Trance Masters · TM002

The Trance Legends Set Chicane, Above & Beyond, ATB & Rank 1

Four melodic masters of trance. From Chicane’s Balearic sunsets, Above & Beyond’s emotional anthems, ATB’s iconic melodies, to the epic power of Rank 1 — these legends defined the heart of trance music.

This 1-hour Ibiza Trance Classics mix is a tribute to the golden age of trance: euphoric builds, lush pads, and unforgettable melodies from the late 90s & early 2000s. Close your eyes, feel the waves, and let the soundtrack of Ibiza sunsets guide you.

Inspired by timeless tracks like: Saltwater, 9PM (Till I Come), Airwave, Sun & Moon…
