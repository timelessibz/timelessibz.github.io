Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC089

Best Classic Trance Mix 1997–2001

Tiesto,Paul van Dyk,Ferry Corsten,Binary Finary,Chicane · ITC089

Step back into the golden era of trance with this Classic Trance Mix featuring the best tracks from the late 90s and early 2000s. A journey through legendary melodies, uplifting energy and timeless anthems that defined the golden age of trance music.

This mix includes iconic tracks from Chicane, Paul van Dyk, Ferry Corsten, Tiësto, Binary Finary, Three Drives, Push, Lost Witness, Nalin & Kane and more. If you love classic trance, uplifting trance, 90s trance and early 2000s trance, this set will bring back the magic of the underground trance scene.

Expect unforgettable anthems like Greece 2000, Offshore, For An Angel, Universal Nation, Out of the Blue and Skydive — tracks that shaped the sound of trance forever.

Perfect for fans of classic trance mixes, old school trance, melodic trance and nostalgic electronic music.
