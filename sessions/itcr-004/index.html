Trance Classics Reworked · ITCR004

16 Legendary Anthems Reimagined For 2026

What if the greatest trance classics of all time were produced today?

Welcome to Trance Classics Reworked a series dedicated to breathing new life into the tracks that shaped a generation.

In this episode, legendary anthems from the golden era of trance are reimagined with modern production, updated sound design and today's dancefloor energy — while preserving the melodies and emotions that made them timeless.

From Rapture and Need To Feel Loved to Synaesthesia Better Off Alone and L'Esperanza , this is where nostalgia meets the future.
