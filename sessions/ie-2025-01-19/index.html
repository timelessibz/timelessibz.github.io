Best of 90s Eurodance

High-Energy Megamix · Gigi D'Agostino, Vengaboys, Aqua & More

Relive the Golden Era of 90s Eurodance! Turn up the volume and get ready for a non-stop, high-energy megamix featuring the most iconic Eurodance hits of the 90s! These are the tracks that made dance floors explode and defined a generation. Whether you're a longtime fan or discovering these classics for the first time, this mix will bring back the ultimate party vibes!

Featuring legendary artists: Gigi D'Agostino, Vengaboys, Aqua, Ann Lee, Sonique, Netzwerk, and more!
