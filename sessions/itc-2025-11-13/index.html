Ibiza Trance Classics

The Golden Era Mix (1998–2006)

Do you remember that one sunset… the one that felt eternal? Let this mix take you right back there — to the golden skies, the salt in the air, and the sound of pure Ibiza trance magic. A journey through euphoric melodies, emotional synths and timeless breakdowns that defined the essence of the Balearic spirit.

Full 1-hour mix of classic trance anthems (1998–2006) featuring: Three Drives, Push, Rank 1, Fred Baker, Svenson, Sunscreem and more legends of the golden era.

Whether you're chasing the last light of the day or driving under starlit skies — this is your soundtrack to freedom.
