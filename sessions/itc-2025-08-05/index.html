Ibiza Trance Classics

Balearic Dreams Chill Trance from the 90s & 2000s (1999-2005)

Welcome to your Balearic dream. This is more than a mix — it’s a journey through the calm, hypnotic, and emotional side of classic trance. Imagine the breeze on your skin, the sound of waves, and melodies that melt into your soul.

This 1-hour session blends Balearic trance, chill progressive, and ambient Ibiza classics from the late 90s to early 2000s — a golden era of warm pads, silky basslines, and floating atmospheres.

Featuring music inspired by legends like: Chicane, Salt Tank, LSG, Solarstone, BT, Sasha, Three Drives, Lost Tribe, and Airwave

Whether you're watching a sunset or dreaming with your eyes closed… this is for you.
