Ibiza Specials

Trance Classics 1997 | Golden Age of Trance

Top 13 Legendary Tracks · The Ultimate Yearbook Mix

Relive the magic of 1997! This mix is a tribute to the golden age of trance, featuring the most legendary instrumental and vocal trance anthems from the era. These tracks defined Ibiza nights, Ministry of Sound, and iconic rave festivals, shaping the sound of early 2000s trance music.

Expect pure nostalgia with: Timeless trance anthems from 1997 Epic uplifting & progressive sounds Influences from Ibiza, Pete Tong & Trance Energy Classic Ministry of Sound & Gatecrasher vibes The foundation of early 2000s trance evolution

Whether you’re a '90s raver or a newcomer to classic trance, this set takes you on a euphoric journey back to one of the most influential years in trance history.
