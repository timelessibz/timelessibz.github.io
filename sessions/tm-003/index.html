Trance Masters · TM003

Lange, York, Cosmic Gate & Vincent De Moor Classic Trance Anthems Mix

Step into the golden age of trance with this TRANCE MASTERS edition featuring four legendary names: Lange, York, Cosmic Gate, and Vincent De Moor. This mix blends euphoric melodies, deep progressive vibes, and iconic anthems that defined the late 90s and early 2000s.

Perfect for trance lovers who crave nostalgia and pure energy on the dancefloor.

Featuring timeless classics like: York – On The Beach, The Awakening & Farewell To The Moon Vincent De Moor – Orion City, Flowtation & Fly Away Cosmic Gate – Exploration of Space, Back to Earth & Fire Wire Lange – Drifting Away, Follow Me & I Believe

Put on your headphones, close your eyes, and relive the true spirit of Ibiza and trance heaven.
