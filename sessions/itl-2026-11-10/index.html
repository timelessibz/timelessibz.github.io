Ibiza Trance Legends

Menno de Jong Tribute Mix — Classic Trance Anthems (2004–2018)

ITL

IBIZA TRANCE LEGENDS presents a 3-Hour Tribute to MENNO DE JONG!

179 minutes of pure golden-era uplifting and progressive trance (2004–2018) celebrating the legendary Dutch DJ, producer, and founder of Intuition Recordings. Featuring 36 timeless originals, collaborations, and iconic remixes including Guanxi, Tundra, Sunset in Rio, Clear Blue Moon, Momentum, Watermark, Ancient Mysteries, Seven Cities, As The Rush Comes, and more.

Turn it up, close your eyes and relive the golden era of trance.

If Menno de Jong's music soundtracked your unforgettable nights, SHARE this tribute with your trance family! Every like, comment and share keeps TIMELESS IBZ alive.
