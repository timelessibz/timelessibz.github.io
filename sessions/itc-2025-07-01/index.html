Ibiza Trance Classics Uplifting Sunset Session 90s–2000s

Feel the magic of Ibiza at golden hour… Close your eyes and imagine yourself on a warm summer night, the waves in the background and a sky turning orange. This uplifting trance sunset session takes you back to the most powerful and melodic moments of the 90s and early 2000s, when trance was more than music — it was emotion, elevation, connection.

Relive the golden age of trance with an hour-long journey through euphoric leads, emotional pads, and unforgettable breakdowns. Featuring classics from OceanLab, Aly & Fila, Airwave, Rank 1, and more

Let this set be the soundtrack to your sunset, wherever you are in the world
