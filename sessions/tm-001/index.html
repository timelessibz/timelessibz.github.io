Trance Masters · TM001

The 4 Legends Set Paul van Dyk, Armin van Buuren, Ferry Corsten & Tiësto

Four trance legends. One unforgettable mix. Step into the golden age of trance with Paul van Dyk, Armin van Buuren, Ferry Corsten, and Tiësto — the pioneers who shaped an entire generation of euphoric dance music.

This 1-hour Ibiza Trance Classics session takes you back to the late 90s & early 2000s — an era of anthemic synths, uplifting melodies, and pure emotional energy. Whether you lived those nights or you’re discovering them for the first time, this is your ticket to trance history.

Featuring iconic sounds, vinyl-era gems, and remixes that defined the global trance movement.
