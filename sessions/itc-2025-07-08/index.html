Ibiza Trance Classics

The Soundtrack of a Generation 90s & 2000s Euphoric Set

The sound that shaped a million memories. This is more than a mix — it’s a journey through time, reliving the golden age of trance with an expertly blended selection of emotional, euphoric and uplifting anthems from the late 90s and early 2000s.

Perfect for driving at night, watching the sunset, or dancing like no one’s watching — this set blends epic breakdowns, iconic melodies and that unmistakable trance energy that defined an era.

Featuring timeless tracks by: Paul van Dyk, System F, Chicane, Solarstone, Kai Tracid, Rank 1, Airwave and many more.

If trance music means something to you — this one’s for you.
