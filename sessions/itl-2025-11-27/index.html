Ibiza Trance Legends

Tiësto Returns to Trance • Classic Trance Mix Tribute

Bring Me To Life & 90s–2000s Hits

Tiësto’s return to trance has awakened a feeling many fans thought was gone forever . After wiping his socials and teasing a mysterious countdown before Dreamstate SoCal, the legend stepped back into the sound that shaped a generation. When he premiered “Bring Me To Life”, it wasn’t just a new track — it felt like the rebirth of the euphoric trance era we all fell in love with.

This tribute mix is my way of celebrating that moment . A journey through his most emotional, uplifting and timeless classics — from the late 90s magic to the early 2000s anthems that defined Tiësto’s legacy. Whether you lived through the golden era or you’re discovering it for the first time, this mix is made to reconnect you with that pure, nostalgic energy .

Close your eyes, wear your best headphones , and let these melodies remind you why trance still touches the soul. Let the pads, the builds, the sparkles and the breakdowns guide you back to the feeling we all miss. The feeling Tiësto just brought back to life.
