Ibiza Specials

Best of 90's Eurodance Mix

Gala, Cher, Fragma, Vengaboys, ATB, Darude, Guru Josh Project

Step back into the golden age of 90s Eurodance with this high-energy mix packed with iconic club anthems, nostalgic synths, and unforgettable vocals. From Technotronic, Gala, Whigfield, ATB, Darude, and more — this set is a celebration of the era that defined dance floors across Europe.

Perfect for workouts, parties, driving, or reliving those unforgettable nights of pure euphoria. Hit Play, turn it Up, and let the rhythm take control.
