90s-2000s Trance Classics Mix (Vinyl Set)

A State of Trance · Armin van Buuren, Above & Beyond

Take a journey back in time with this incredible 90s Trance Classics Mix, showcasing legendary artists like Armin van Buuren, Tiesto, and Ferry Corsten. This vinyl-only set pays tribute to the golden age of trance, bringing you right into the heart of the unforgettable rave culture and festivals of the 1990s.

Dive into a seamless mix of uplifting trance, vocal trance, and progressive trance hits that defined the decade. For every true A STATE OF TRANCE fan, this mix is an essential listen, capturing the spirit of ASOT, Tomorrowland, and other iconic electronic music events. Close your eyes, relax, and let the nostalgic energy take you back to the magic of 90s trance. With album-length perfection, this mix fuses classic melodies and old-school techno vibes, ensuring you'll be dancing like it's 1999 all over again.
