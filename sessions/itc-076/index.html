Ibiza Trance Classics · ITC076

The Most Legendary Ibiza Anthems Ever!

Chicane, PVD, Ferry Corsten

You’ve just found THE mix that defines the golden era of Ibiza trance. These are not just tracks — they’re memories, sunsets, and goosebumps.

IBIZA TRANCE CLASSICS brings together the most eternal anthems of all time, featuring Chicane, Paul van Dyk, Ferry Corsten, Mike Koglin, Tilt, Agnelli & Nelson, and more.

Every melody here shaped the sound of Ibiza forever — you’ll feel every second of it. If you remember these tunes… you lived the real era.
